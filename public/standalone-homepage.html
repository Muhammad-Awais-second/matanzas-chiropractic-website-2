Featured Non-Invasive Care

Shockwave Therapy (ESWT) for Persistent Tendon Pain

Shockwave Therapy (ESWT) uses non-invasive acoustic pressure waves that may help reduce pain and improve function. It is designed for people whose symptoms persist after conservative care (stretching, physical therapy, orthotics, activity modification).

5 Conditions Addressed with Shockwave Therapy:

  • 01. Plantar fasciitis — Persistent heel and arch pain.
  • 02. Tennis elbow — Lateral elbow tendon pain and grip discomfort.
  • 03. Achilles tendinopathy — Heel cord and lower calf tendon discomfort.
  • 04. Patellar tendinopathy (jumper's knee) — Knee tendon pain below the kneecap.
  • 05. Calcific shoulder tendinopathy — Persistent shoulder tendon discomfort.
